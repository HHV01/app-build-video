# Bộ prompt hình ảnh — MILO và chiếc xe tuổi thơ

## Khóa nhân vật và phong cách

Dùng đoạn này ở đầu mọi prompt:

```text
Use the supplied schoolboy character sheet as the strict identity reference. Keep exactly the same protagonist in every image: Vietnamese schoolboy mascot, large round white face, messy dark-brown side-swept hair, tiny black line eyes, rosy cheeks, simple thin black limbs, black mitten hands and oval shoes, white short-sleeve school shirt, black shorts, vivid red pioneer scarf. Preserve facial proportions, hair silhouette, costume colors and line weight. Charming hand-drawn 2D chibi cartoon, bold slightly organic black outlines, flat clean colors, subtle soft shading, expressive comic symbols, playful educational YouTube animation, polished editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape, clear foreground/midground/background separation, no photorealism, no 3D, no watermark, no random text, no malformed hands, no duplicate protagonist.
```

Ghi chú sản xuất: tạo logo và chữ MILO riêng ở hậu kỳ để bảo đảm chính xác; trong ảnh AI chỉ cần bao bì và xe màu xanh đúng nhận diện tổng quát.

## Prompt từng cảnh

### 01 — Sân trường bình thường

```text
A calm Vietnamese primary-school courtyard during morning class, cream school building, red-tile roof, leafy trees, empty basketball court, a few students studying through open classroom windows. The protagonist sits at a wooden desk looking slightly bored. Wide establishing shot, peaceful mood, soft morning sunlight, lots of clean negative space.
```

### 02 — Phát hiện chiếc xe xanh

```text
Inside a Vietnamese classroom, one excited classmate suddenly points through the open window toward a bright green promotional drink truck entering the school gate. The protagonist snaps upright with huge surprised eyes and a red comic exclamation mark. Dynamic three-quarter composition, diagonal motion lines, classmates turning their heads at once.
```

### 03 — Tin MILO lan khắp lớp

```text
The protagonist whispers excitedly to a desk mate that free chocolate malt drinks have arrived, both children glowing with anticipation. A humorous visual chain reaction spreads across the classroom as students pass the message row by row, speech bubbles represented only by green truck and drink-box icons, no written words. Medium-wide shot, escalating comic energy.
```

### 04 — Xin xuống phòng y tế

```text
The protagonist stands dramatically beside his desk, one hand on his forehead pretending to feel weak and the other hand raised toward the teacher, while imagining a glowing spoonful of malt powder inside a thought bubble. The teacher gives him a skeptical side-eye. Comedic staging, classmates suppressing laughter, warm classroom palette.
```

### 05 — Cả trường bùng nổ

```text
School bell rings and dozens of simplified chibi students rush into the courtyard toward the green truck. The protagonist leads the stampede with an ecstatic grin, red scarf flying behind him. High-energy wide shot, exaggerated speed lines, dust puffs, colorful comic bursts, safe playful chaos.
```

### 06 — Câu hỏi trung tâm

```text
Hero composition: the protagonist stands in front of the mysterious green school truck, holding a magnifying glass toward a small chocolate malt drink carton. Around him float a question mark, school bell, running shoe, cacao pod and childhood-memory sparkles. Dark-to-bright vignette, investigative but playful mood, clear center space for title added later.
```

### 07 — MILO là một ký ức

